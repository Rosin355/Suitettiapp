## Digital Yogin Publication Strategy

The app will be published under the **Digital Yogin** Apple Developer account once
the app is feature-complete (v1.0) and has passed internal testing.

Steps before submission:

1. Create an App Store Connect record under Digital Yogin's team.
2. Update `PRODUCT_BUNDLE_IDENTIFIER` to `com.digitalyogin.DiteloSuiTetti` (or the
   agreed bundle ID) in Xcode → Target → Build Settings.
3. Update `DEVELOPMENT_TEAM` to the Digital Yogin team ID in signing settings.
4. Re-provision certificates and provisioning profiles under the new team.
5. Confirm Privacy Nutrition Labels: the app currently collects no user data.

## App Transfer Considerations

Apple supports transferring an app between developer accounts via App Store Connect
("Transfer App"). Key rules at time of writing (2026):

- The app must have at least **one approved version** in the store before transfer is allowed.
- Both source and destination accounts must be enrolled in the Apple Developer Program.
- Transfer is **irreversible** once initiated.
- In-app purchases, subscriptions, and ratings transfer with the app.
- Provisioning profiles must be regenerated by the new team after transfer.

## Capabilities to Avoid Before Transfer (Unless Strictly Necessary)

Adding these capabilities before the transfer significantly complicates or blocks it:

| Capability | Risk |
|---|---|
| Push Notifications (APNs) | Requires re-registration under new team cert; breaks existing device tokens |
| iCloud (CloudKit / Documents) | CloudKit containers are team-scoped and cannot be transferred |
| Keychain Sharing | Shared keychain groups are team-prefixed; data inaccessible after transfer |
| Sign in with Apple | Apple ID associations are account-scoped; existing users may lose access |
| App Groups | Group IDs are team-prefixed; extension sharing breaks after transfer |

**Current status:** None of the above capabilities are enabled. Keep it this way until
after the App Store transfer is complete.
